Dart counter, X01, statistics and darts modes

This is the public MULTISPORTS SCORING hub for discovering Darts features before opening the app.

Dart counterUse MULTISPORTS SCORING as a dart counter for X01, Cricket, Killer, Challenge and other modes, with player profiles, match history and statistics.
X01Score X01 darts games in 301, 501, 701 or 901 with MULTISPORTS SCORING, including in/out options, sets, legs, undo and statistics.
CricketScore Cricket darts with targets 15 to 20 and Bull, tracking closes, points and match statistics.
KillerPlay Killer darts with player numbers, lives, Killer phase, eliminations, bots and match tracking in MULTISPORTS SCORING.
ShanghaiTrack a Shanghai darts game with rounds, singles, doubles, triples and scoring in MULTISPORTS SCORING.
ChallengeMULTISPORTS SCORING Challenge tracks S, D, T, Bull and MISS results on configurable targets for solo or multiplayer darts sessions.
Darts PokerDarts Poker combines darts with card-hand mechanics in MULTISPORTS SCORING, tracking hands, targets and turns.
Gros 6Discover Gros 6 in MULTISPORTS SCORING, a darts mode with Gros/Petit target zones, guided setup and match tracking.
CRADOSCRADOS is an original arcade darts mode in MULTISPORTS SCORING with a gauge, custom hit rules, AI bots and statistics.
X01 trainingTrain X01 darts with MULTISPORTS SCORING and track sessions, performance and progress over time.
Darts statisticsReview darts statistics, match history, player profiles and records in MULTISPORTS SCORING.
Online X01MULTISPORTS SCORING includes an online X01 flow for remote darts matches while keeping score and match tracking.
